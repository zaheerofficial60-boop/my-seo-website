In Detail

What on-page SEO work actually covers, page by page.

A good page answers one clear search, in the order a visitor expects to read it. Each element below is worked through so the topic, the location and the next step are obvious to Google and your customer.

T

Title and Meta Writing

Each title is written for one main keyword and one clear promise. Meta descriptions summarise the page in plain words and give people in Layyah and nearby towns a reason to choose your result.

H

Heading Hierarchy

One H1 states the page topic, H2 headings split it into useful sections, and H3 headings answer smaller questions underneath, so the outline of the page reads clearly from top to bottom.

K

Keyword Placement

Your main keyword belongs in the title, opening paragraph, one early heading and naturally through the text. Supporting phrases are added where they help the reader, not repeated until the page sounds forced.

↗

Internal Linking Structure

Service pages link to related services, location pages and helpful blog posts with descriptive anchor text. Important pages receive links from more places, while unrelated pages are not forced into confusing links.

IMG

Image Optimization

Images are renamed before upload, given honest alt text describing what they show, and sized for the space they fill. Every important image supports the words around it instead of slowing the page down.

C

Content Improvement

Short answers are expanded, repeated paragraphs are merged, and missing details such as services and areas served are added where needed. The finished text still sounds like your business, only clearer and easier to trust.

On a Real Website

How the work is done on your live website.

Nothing is changed blindly. Each page is opened, read and checked in the same way a customer and a search engine would see it.

01

Open and Read

The page is read fully first, checking whether the first screen explains what you offer, where you offer it, and how a visitor can call or email you.

02

Check the Code

Title, meta description, headings, image alt text and internal links are checked in the page code, and compared with the keyword planned for that page.

03

Make Careful Edits

Changes are applied one page at a time, keeping your layout, colours and wording style intact. Links are tested after editing so nothing points to a missing page.

04

Recheck and Record

The finished page is opened again on desktop and mobile views, every change is noted, and anything needing new writing or a better image is listed clearly.

Common Problems

On-page mistakes this service fixes.

Many local websites lose visibility for simple reasons inside the page itself. These problems are found and corrected during this work.

T

Missing or Copied Titles

Pages sharing one title give Google no reason to treat them as different answers. Each page receives its own accurate title.

H

Duplicate Headings

Repeated H1 headings blur the topic. Headings are rewritten so each level adds new, useful information.

C

Thin Content

A few lines cannot answer a search properly. Short pages gain real service details, local context and clear next steps.

K

Keyword Stuffing

The same phrase in every sentence reads badly. Overused wording becomes natural sentences that still cover the topic fully.

↗

Broken Internal Links

Links to deleted pages or old addresses waste visits. Every internal link is checked, repaired or replaced.

F

Unclear Page Focus

One page selling every service to every town says nothing clearly. Pages are refocused on one service at a time.

FAQ

Common questions about On-Page SEO.

How long does an on-page SEO project take?

A typical 10–40 page website can take around 2–3 weeks for a full optimization pass, with priority pages handled first.

Will you change my website design?

No. The focus is on SEO elements such as copy, tags, structure, links and images. Design changes are only suggested when they affect usability or SEO.

Do you also write new content?

Yes, where needed. Full content planning and SEO copywriting are also available as a separate service.

Can on-page SEO improve Google rankings?

It can strengthen relevance and page quality signals. Rankings also depend on competition, technical SEO, content quality, authority and other factors.

Can you optimise just one page first?

Yes. One important page, such as your homepage or main service page, can be optimised first so you can see the changes clearly before deciding on the rest of the website.
