FAQ

Common questions about On-Page SEO.

How long does an on-page SEO project take?

A typical 10–40 page website can take around 2–3 weeks for a full optimization pass, with priority pages handled first.

Will you change my website design?

No. The focus is on SEO elements such as copy, tags, structure, links and images. Design changes are only suggested when they affect usability or SEO.

Do you also write new content?

Yes, where needed. Full content planning and SEO copywriting are also available as a separate service.

Can on-page SEO improve Google rankings?

It can strengthen relevance and page quality signals. Rankings also depend on competition, technical SEO, content quality, authority and other factors.
