FAQ

Common Off-Page SEO questions.

What is Off-Page SEO?

Off-page SEO includes activities outside your website that can strengthen authority, reputation and visibility, especially through relevant links and brand mentions.

Do you use paid or spammy backlinks?

I focus on quality and relevance rather than bulk spam. Every opportunity should have a clear reason to exist and provide value to users.

How many backlinks do I need?

There is no useful fixed number. The right target depends on your niche, competitors, current authority, link quality and the strength of the pages you want to rank.

Can link building guarantee rankings?

No SEO provider can honestly guarantee a specific ranking. Link building is one part of a broader strategy alongside technical SEO, content, on-page optimization and search intent.
