Audit in Detail

What an audit checks, area by area.

A good audit looks at the whole website, because small problems in several places add up to one big visibility problem.

TH

Technical Health

I check whether search engines can crawl and index your important pages, and look for broken links, redirect chains and duplicate URLs that quietly waste visibility.

PE

On-Page Elements

Every important page is reviewed for title, headings, wording and search intent. Pages targeting the wrong topic, repeating each other or leaving their subject unclear are flagged.

CQ

Content Quality

I review whether your content answers what customers search for. Thin pages, outdated information, missing service details and topics buyers care about but you never cover are recorded.

IL

Internal Linking

Internal links tell search engines which pages matter most. I look for important pages buried too deep, pages with almost no links, and chances to link related services together.

LP

Local Presence and GBP

For local businesses I review location signals and how they line up with your Google Business Profile, including service areas and local landing pages.

MS

Mobile, Speed and Backlinks

I check how the site works on mobile phones, how quickly pages load, and the overall backlink profile at a high level, so slow pages and weak authority are not missed.

Competitor Analysis

How competitor analysis turns rivals into a roadmap.

Competitor analysis is not about copying another business. It is about understanding why other websites appear ahead of you, and which advantages you can match or beat.

Finding Your Real Competitors

1Identifying the websites that actually appear for your services and locations, often different from businesses you compete with offline
2Comparing the keywords those websites are visible for, and the pages that earn that visibility
3Studying their content, service pages and location pages to see what search engines reward
4Reviewing their local visibility and how their websites support the areas they want to be found for

FAQ

Common SEO audit questions.

What is an SEO audit?

An SEO audit is a structured review of a website's technical setup, pages, content, internal links, authority and other factors that can influence organic search performance.

How often should a website be audited?

A full audit can be useful when launching a new SEO campaign, after major website changes, or whenever performance has stalled. Smaller checks can be performed more regularly.

Will an audit guarantee higher rankings?

No. An audit identifies opportunities and problems; ranking improvements depend on implementation, competition, search demand, site quality and many other factors.

Do you audit local business websites?

Yes. Local businesses can be reviewed for website SEO, location relevance, service pages, local content and their broader local search strategy.

What will I receive after an audit?

You will receive a clear written report that lists the problems found, explains why each one matters in plain language, compares your website with your main search competitors, and ends with a priority action list showing what to fix first.
