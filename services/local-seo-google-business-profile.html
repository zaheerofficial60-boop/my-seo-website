FAQ

Common Local SEO & GBP questions.

What is Google Business Profile optimization?

It is the process of improving an eligible business profile so its information is accurate, useful and aligned with the business and its local customers.

Can you guarantee the #1 Maps position?

No. Local rankings vary by location, competition, relevance, prominence and other factors. Good optimization improves the signals you can control.

Do reviews help local SEO?

Reviews can contribute to customer trust and are one part of the broader local search ecosystem. Genuine customer feedback is always preferable to artificial reviews.

Do you work with businesses outside Layyah?

Yes. Layyah is the home base, but local SEO can be planned for other cities and service areas depending on the business model and target market.
