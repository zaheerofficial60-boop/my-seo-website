FAQ

Common keyword research questions.

Why is keyword research important?

It helps you understand how people search, what they want and which topics or services deserve dedicated pages. That makes SEO more focused.

Is search volume the most important metric?

No. Relevance, search intent, competition and business value can matter more than a large volume number that does not match your customers.

What is keyword mapping?

Keyword mapping connects target search terms to specific URLs so each important query has a clear page and your website has a logical structure.

Can you research local keywords?

Yes. Local keyword research can include city, neighborhood, service-area and location-intent searches for businesses targeting specific markets.
