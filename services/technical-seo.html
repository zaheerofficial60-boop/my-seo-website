In Detail

What a technical SEO service actually checks, in detail.

Good content and links cannot do their job if the technical base is weak. This deeper review looks at the systems underneath your pages, not just the words on them, and turns possible issues into clear, practical fixes.

CR

Crawlability and Indexation Checks

I trace how crawlers move through your site, from the homepage to your deepest service and blog pages. Pages that are buried, orphaned or blocked by weak internal linking are flagged, so important URLs stay easy to discover and index.

IX

XML Sitemap and Robots.txt Review

Your sitemap should list only live, indexable pages you want in search results. I check it for dead links, redirected URLs and missing key pages, and review robots.txt for rules that may accidentally block important pages or files.

⚡

Site Speed and Core Web Vitals

Slow pages frustrate visitors and make crawling harder. I look at loading speed, responsiveness and visual stability, and identify heavy images, blocking scripts and layout shifts. Fixes are ordered by impact, so helpful changes come first.

M

Mobile-Friendliness Checks

Many visitors browse on phones, so your site must work properly on small screens. I check tap targets, readable text and responsive layouts, and whether mobile pages carry the same content and links as desktop pages.

🔒

HTTPS and Security Basics

A secure connection protects visitors and is a basic expectation for any business website. I check that HTTPS works on every page, old HTTP addresses redirect cleanly, and mixed content warnings are not undermining trust.

SC

Structured Data, Redirects and Canonical Tags

Small tag and redirect mistakes can quietly split or hide pages. I review canonical tags, clean up redirect chains and loops, and correct structured data so search engines read your business and services clearly.

Common Problems

Technical problems this service is built to solve.

Many technical issues stay invisible until traffic drops or new pages refuse to appear in Google. These are the problems business owners across Layyah, Chobara, Mianwali, Chowk Azam, Jaman Shah, Dera Ghazi Khan and Karor Lal Eason most often bring to me.

IX

Pages Google Cannot Index

A page that is blocked, marked noindex by mistake or left out of the sitemap may never appear in search, however good its content is. I find why important pages are excluded and correct the signals.

⚡

Slow Loading on Mobile

Pages that load slowly on mobile connections lose visitors before the content is seen. I pinpoint the images, scripts and responses slowing a page down and recommend practical changes that make it lighter and faster.

↻

Duplicate URLs and Crawl Errors

The same page reachable at several addresses can split its signals, while dead pages and broken links waste crawl attention. Canonical tags, clean redirects and repaired links bring order back to the site.

FAQ

Common Technical SEO questions.

What is Technical SEO?

Technical SEO improves the technical foundation of a website so search engines can crawl, understand and index its pages effectively.

Can Technical SEO improve rankings?

Fixing technical barriers can help search engines access and understand your content. Rankings also depend on content, relevance, authority, competition and other factors.

Do you fix website speed?

Yes. I audit performance and identify practical opportunities around images, scripts, resources, layout stability and other page-speed factors.

Do I need a technical SEO audit?

An audit is especially useful when a site has indexing problems, traffic drops, migrations, many URLs, slow pages or unclear technical issues.

Do you check XML sitemaps and robots.txt files?

Yes. I review your XML sitemap to make sure it lists the correct, indexable pages, and I check your robots.txt file for rules that may accidentally block important pages, images or scripts that search engines need to crawl.
