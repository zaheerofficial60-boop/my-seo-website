FAQ

Common Technical SEO questions.

What is Technical SEO?

Technical SEO improves the technical foundation of a website so search engines can crawl, understand and index its pages effectively.

Can Technical SEO improve rankings?

Fixing technical barriers can help search engines access and understand your content. Rankings also depend on content, relevance, authority, competition and other factors.

Do you fix website speed?

Yes. I audit performance and identify practical opportunities around images, scripts, resources, layout stability and other page-speed factors.

Do I need a technical SEO audit?

An audit is especially useful when a site has indexing problems, traffic drops, migrations, many URLs, slow pages or unclear technical issues.
